# Workshop Discovery & Planning

This skill guides the initial information gathering to begin to plan a workshop. This includes strategic goals, problem statements, desired outcomes, stakeholders, logistics (duration, location, number of attendees, hybrid vs. in-person).

## Context & Desired Outcome

### Project Sponsorship
1. Executive Sponsor: [Name,Title,Contact]
2. Primary Point of Contact: [Name,Title,Contact]
3. Who are the people involved on the customer side and what are their roles?
4. Who are the people involved on the Google side and what are their roles?

### The "Why" (Context)
1. Background: Why do we want to hold a workshop with this customer? [Freeform text - Describe the context and reason for this workshop]

2. Problem Statement: What specific challenge are we trying to solve? [Freeform text - Describe the problem statement]

### Desired Outcomes
1. Workshop Objective: What would make the workshop successful from the perspective of the customer? [Freeform text]

What would make the workshop successful from the perspective of the Google Cloud Account Team?[Freeform text]

2. Workshop Outputs: What outputs should be generated by the end of the workshop? [Freeform text]

3. Success Metrics: How will we know the workshop was successful 30 days after it ends? Who owns the follow-up activities after the workshop?

4. Success Barriers: What are the biggest risks that could derail this workshop?

### Participant Profile
1. Target Audience: [C-Suite, VP, IT, line of business, cross-functional, etc.]
2. Estimated Headcount: [Number]
3. Google VIPs Required: [List names/roles]
4. Key Subject Matter Experts (SMEs) Required: [List names/roles]
5. Diversity of Perspective: Are all necessary departments represented (Sales, Engineering, Design, Legal)?

### Logistical Requirements
1. Timing & Duration
   - Proposed Dates: [Date Range]
   - Duration: [e.g., Half-day, 2 full days, 3-hour sprint]
   - Timezone Considerations: [If virtual/hybrid]

2. Venue
   - Delivery Mode: [In-Person / Virtual / Hybrid]
   - Platform (If Virtual): [e.g., Miro/Mural for whiteboarding, Zoom/Teams for breakout rooms]
   - Physical Space (If In-Person): [Requirements: Moveable furniture, wall space for Post-its, catering]

3. Technical Environment & Access
   - If users will be building in a Google Cloud environment, will they use Quiklabs, a sandbox environment in their Google Cloud Organization, or something else?

4. Budget
   - What is the budget for the workshop? [e.g., $5k, $10k, $20k]
   - Who will be responsible for handling the logistics?

### Workshop Format & Methodology
Please indicate the desired focus areas for the agenda.

A. Learning & Inspiration
[ ] Industry Thought Leaders: Keynote speakers or external experts to provide market context.
[ ] Demos & Case Studies: Showing "the art of the possible" through existing solutions or competitor analysis.

B. Design Thinking & Problem Solving
[ ] Problem Identification: Root cause analysis and defining the "How Might We" statements.
[ ] User Needs Discovery: Empathy mapping and persona development.
[ ] Ideation: High-volume brainstorming and creative solutioning.
[ ] Use Case Prioritization: Sorting ideas by feasibility vs. impact (e.g., 2x2 matrix).

C. Business Case & Execution
[ ] Value Case Building: Quantifying the ROI or strategic value of selected solutions.
[ ] Prototyping: Low-fidelity sketching or wireframing of the solution.
[ ] Roadmapping: High-level timeline and resource identification for next steps.


### Inputs
1. Existng Materials: What existing strategy documents, design documents, user interviews, data, or reports already exist?
2. Research: What information should we gather prior to the workshop via stakeholder interviews, customer interviews, or surveys?
3. Pre-Work: Do participants need to come to the workshop having already prepared anything (e.g. list of pain points, user interviews, user personas, user journeys, ideas, opportunity areas)
4. Materials Needed: [e.g., Printed workbooks, specific software access, physical prototypes]
