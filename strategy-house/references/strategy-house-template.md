# Strategy House Template: A Guide to Strategic Planning

This template will guide your team through the process of building a comprehensive strategy house, ensuring alignment from vision to execution. The strategy house provides a visual framework to connect your overarching Vision with actionable Focus Areas, Prioritization efforts, Measurement of success, and Impacted Teams.

## Section 1: Vision (Our Why)
The Vision forms the roof of your strategy house. It defines your ultimate purpose and what success looks like at the highest level.

**Guiding Question:**
Why is this important to our overall strategy for the organization?

**Prompts:**
1. Define your overarching organizational vision:
   - What is the long-term aspiration for the company?
   - How does this strategy house contribute directly to achieving that overarching vision?
2. Articulate the core problem you are solving or the core value you are creating:
   - What fundamental need or opportunity does this strategic effort address?
3. Describe the desired future state once this strategy is successfully implemented:
   - What will be different? Who will benefit?

## Section 2: Strategy & Business Focus Areas
These are the pillars supporting your vision. Focus Areas represent the key strategic initiatives or themes that must be addressed to realize your vision.

**Guiding Question:**
What outcomes need to be delivered for the transformation to be successful? (e.g., improve release time)

**Template Prompts:** For each Focus Area, consider the following:
- Focus Area: [Name of Focus Area]
- What specific, high-level outcome do we aim to achieve in this area?
- Why is this Focus Area critical to our overall strategy? What problem are we trying to solve?
- What are the current challenges or opportunities within this area?

## Section 3: Prioritization (Phases)
Once your focus areas are defined, you need to determine the most effective order to tackle the work.

**Guiding Questions:**
- What are the major jobs to be done and most effective in order to complete them?
- How do you measure success within each phase?

**Template Prompts:** For each Phase, outline the key activities and expected outcomes.
- Phase 1: [Name of Phase/Timeframe]
- What are the primary objectives for this phase?
- What key activities or milestones must be completed?
- What dependencies exist for starting or completing this phase?
- What is the first milestone that will set us up for success?

## Section 4: Measure Success (OKRs/KPIs)
This section focuses on defining how you will track progress.

**Guiding Questions:**
- How do we know what good looks like?
- What are we directionally aiming for? Aim for data driven outcomes.

**Template Prompts:** For each Phase, define clear Objectives and Key Results (OKRs) or Key Performance Indicators (KPIs).
- **Objective:** [Specific, inspiring goal]
- **Key Result 1:** [Measurable, time-bound result]
- **Current Metric:** [Baseline value]
- **Target Metric:** [Desired value]

## Section 5: Impacted Teams
Identifying the teams that will be involved in or affected by this strategic change.

**Guiding Question:**
Which teams are going to be accountable for the change?
- **Team(s):** [Name of Team]
- How will this/these team(s) be directly impacted (e.g., new processes, tools, responsibilities)?
- What role will this/these team(s) play in the execution of the strategy?
- What support or resources will this/these team(s) need?
