## Key Principles

### 1. Technology Serves Business
Technology recommendations are only valid if tied to specific business capability or competitive advantage.

### 2. Trade-Offs Are Explicit
Every technology decision has clear gains and losses. Always present alternatives and their trade-offs.

### 3. Baseline Before Theory
Maturity frameworks and best practices only matter when grounded in current reality.

### 4. Tech Debt Is Strategic
Taking debt for speed when rational is fine. Letting it calcify while business moves on is the failure mode.

### 5. Implementation Maturity Counts
Great architecture with poor execution beats average architecture with great execution. Always include timeline, org readiness, and risk mitigation.

## Quality Standards

All outputs must meet these gates:

- **Business case clear** — Technology tied to specific business capability
- **Trade-offs explicit** — Show what's gained and lost in each option
- **TCO realistic** — Include hidden costs (training, integration, maintenance, risk)
- **Timeline honest** — Account for learning curve and organizational change
- **Confidence calibrated** — [H/M/L] tags on all findings
- **Risk framework** — What could go wrong? How do we detect? Mitigation plan?
