# Multi-Skill Workflows & Business Playbooks

**Purpose:** 15 business problem playbooks showing which skills activate in what order for common scenarios. Context flow management, bidirectional dependencies, and scenario framework application.

**Version:** 5.0 | **Updated:** 2026-03-10

---

## Overview

Each playbook triggers on a specific business problem and shows the exact sequence of skills, timelines, decision points, and success metrics. Playbooks are templates—adjust timeline and depth (Quick Strike / Standard / Deep Dive) based on user urgency.

## Playbook 1: Revenue Growth Problem

**Trigger:** "Revenue is stalled / we need to grow faster / growth is decelerating"

**Speed Mode Default:** Standard (if urgent, can compress to Quick Strike; if multi-year, use Deep Dive)

**Scenario Framework:** All scenarios (Upside = growth accelerates; Downside = market slows or competition intensifies; Disruptive = new competitor or market shift)

**Primary Timeline (Standard Mode):** 5-7 business days

**Sequence:**
1. **Strategy Partner (1 day):** MECE Issue Tree (isolate growth blocker: market demand, competitive position, execution, or business model?). Hypothesis Engine on growth levers. Decision frame on growth path. Output: Growth opportunity map with 2-3 hypotheses ranked by impact + confidence.

2. **Growth Strategy (1.5 days):** Market sizing for each path (TAM/SAM/SOM). Growth curve modeling. Unit economics by lever. Scenario modeling. Output: Growth model showing revenue potential by path + CAC/LTV assumptions.

3. **Rumelt Forge (1 day):** Forge growth strategy from diagnosis. Create kernel (crux: fundamental growth opportunity? guiding policy: how do we win?). Output: Strategy kernel with 3-5 coherent actions.

4. **GTM Architect (1 day):** Design customer acquisition motion for growth path. Customer segmentation and positioning. Sales/marketing motion. Output: GTM playbook with customer target, value prop, channels, sales process.

5. **Financial Strategy (1 day):** Model unit economics of growth levers (CAC, LTV, payback, margin by lever). 3-5 year revenue projection. Scenario modeling. Output: Financial model with growth path comparison.

6. **People & Talent (0.5 days):** Headcount model for growth execution (sales, marketing, product, operations). Talent acquisition plan. Output: Talent plan with hiring roadmap.

7. **Operating Model (0.5 days, if needed):** Org design for growth execution if scaling requires org changes. Sales/marketing structure. Output: Org structure for growth team.

**Decision Point after Phase 1:** Which growth vector (organic penetration / adjacent segment / acquisition / combination)? Probability of success?

**Key Risk Flags:**
- Growth opportunity overstated (validate CAC/LTV with market data)
- Competitive response underestimated
- Execution capability insufficient
- Market demand assumption incorrect

**Kill Conditions:**
- If LTV:CAC ratio <3:1, growth is uneconomical; pivot required
- If market growth rate <3%, organic growth path may be limited
- If competitive response faster than execution plan, growth path endangered

**Success Metrics:**
- Growth strategy selected with 80%+ team confidence
- Financial model shows path to profitability
- GTM playbook is actionable with clear CAC/LTV targets
- Talent plan realistic and resourced
- Market opportunity confirmed through primary customer research

## Playbook 2: Competitive Threat / Positioning

**Trigger:** "We're losing share to a competitor / a new player is winning / we're being outmaneuvered"

**Speed Mode Default:** Standard (Quick Strike if immediate threat; Deep Dive if existential)

**Scenario Framework:** Base/Downside/Disruptive (how does competitor success accelerate Downside or Disruptive?)

**Primary Timeline (Standard Mode):** 4-5 business days

**Sequence:**
1. **Strategy Partner (1 day):** Competitive Landscape (deep competitor profile: strategy, positioning, capabilities, customer value prop). Strategic Position assessment. Win/Loss analysis. Scenario Planning. Output: Competitive threat assessment + positioning options.

2. **AI-Native Strategy (0.5 days, parallel):** Real-time competitive intelligence. Market sentiment analysis. Output: Current competitive momentum assessment.

3. **Rumelt Forge (1 day):** Forge positioning strategy. Create kernel (crux: defensible differentiation? guiding policy: how do we compete?). Test resilience in Downside. Output: Positioning strategy with coherent actions.

4. **GTM Architect (1 day):** Design go-to-market to exploit positioning advantage. Customer positioning and messaging vs. competitor. Sales strategy to defend/attack. Output: GTM playbook for competitive positioning.

5. **Operating Model (0.5 days):** Reorganize to focus resources on defensible positions. Win/loss review and accountability. Output: Org structure for competitive execution.

6. **Financial Strategy (0.5 days):** Price strategy to defend/attack. Margin impact. Output: Pricing and margin strategy.

7. **Product Innovation (0.5 days, if needed):** Product roadmap to defend/attack vs. competitor. Feature priorities. Output: Product strategy for competitive positioning.

**Decision Point after Phase 1:** Head-to-head competition in all segments, or find white space to dominate? What's our defensible position?

**Success Metrics:**
- Clear positioning articulated (what we do different/better)
- Defensible strategy with 80%+ confidence
- GTM motion aligned with positioning
- Win/loss metrics tracked monthly
- Market share stabilization or recovery plan with timeline

## Playbook 3: Cost Transformation / Efficiency

**Trigger:** "Margins are compressed / cash burn is too high / we need to reduce costs / we're not profitable"

**Speed Mode Default:** Standard (Quick Strike if immediate cash crisis; Deep Dive if multi-function)

**Scenario Framework:** Base/Downside (when are we forced to be efficient?)

**Primary Timeline (Standard Mode):** 5-7 business days

**Sequence:**
1. **Strategy Partner (1 day):** Value Chain analysis. Competitive benchmarking. MECE Issue Tree. Decision frame on cost approach (operational efficiency vs. structural redesign vs. outsourcing vs. combination). Output: Cost opportunity map with 2-3 levers ranked by impact.

2. **Operating Model (1.5 days):** Design lean operating model. Function-by-function cost analysis. Automation/digitization opportunities. Output: Lean operating model with cost targets per function.

3. **Rumelt Forge (1 day):** Forge cost strategy. Create kernel (crux: what cost structure required? guiding policy: how achieve it?). Validate kernel doesn't harm growth/positioning. Output: Strategy kernel for cost transformation.

4. **Financial Strategy (1 day):** Model post-transformation cost structure. Timeline to target costs. Impact on EBITDA. Scenario modeling. Output: Cost reduction roadmap with financial targets and timeline.

5. **People & Talent (1 day):** Design talent model for efficient operations. Organizational redesign implications. Transition planning. Output: Talent plan for efficiency transformation.

6. **Technology Strategy (0.5 days):** Automate/digitize cost drivers. Systems consolidation. Output: Tech roadmap for cost reduction.

7. **GTM Architect (0.5 days, if applicable):** If cost driven by GTM efficiency, redesign sales/marketing model. Output: GTM efficiency improvements.

**Decision Point after Phase 1:** Operational efficiency (optimize existing) or structural redesign (change model)? Pace (quick cuts or phased)?

**Success Metrics:**
- EBITDA margin improvement to target (e.g., +5 percentage points)
- Cost reduction achieved per plan
- Employee retention >90%
- Customer satisfaction stable or improving
- Growth rate not impacted

## Playbook 6: Organizational Redesign / Restructuring

**Trigger:** "Our org is slow / misaligned / not set up for where we're going / we need to scale"

## Playbook 9: Turnaround / Crisis Response

**Trigger:** "The business is in trouble / we're losing money / we're bleeding cash / we have 6-12 months to fix this"

**Speed Mode Default:** Quick Strike → Standard

**Scenario Framework:** Downside/Base (conservative assumptions)

**Primary Timeline (Quick Strike):** 3-5 business days for diagnosis + immediate action; then Standard for detailed plan

**Phase 1 (Quick Strike - Days 1-2):**

1. **Strategy Partner (0.5 days):** MECE Issue Tree rapid decomposition. Hypothesis Engine on quickest levers. Decision frame on stabilization. Output: Problem diagnosis + stabilization hypothesis.

2. **Financial Strategy (0.5 days):** Cash runway analysis. Burn rate analysis. Quick financial modeling of cost cuts. Output: Cash timeline + stabilization targets.

3. **Rumelt Forge (0.5 days):** Rapid turnaround strategy. Output: Quick kernel for immediate action.

4. **Leadership decision:** Which stabilization approach? Immediate actions (first 30 days). Output: Crisis action plan for first month.

**Phase 2 (Standard - Days 3-7):**

5. **Operating Model (1 day):** FAST org redesign. Quick wins identification. Output: Cost reduction plan + org redesign.

6. **GTM Architect (1 day, if revenue issue):** FAST customer engagement or win-back strategy. At-risk customer save strategy. Output: Revenue stabilization plan.

7. **People & Talent (0.5 days):** Rapid talent decisions. Leadership communication. Retention of essential talent. Output: People transition plan.

8. **Financial Strategy (1 day):** Model path to cash flow positive. Capital needs identification. Output: Financial stability roadmap.

**Decision Points:**
- **Day 1:** Problem diagnosis (growth? margins? cash?)
- **Day 2:** Stabilization approach selected
- **Day 5:** Detailed 90-day turnaround plan
- **Day 7:** Board approval of turnaround plan

**Success Metrics:**
- Cash runway extended (6 months to 12+ months)
- Path to profitability clear (break-even within 18 months)
- Key talent retained (>90% of essential team)
- Top 5 customers retained (>95% retention)
- Monthly burn rate declining

## Speed Mode Coordination Rule

When one skill is Quick Strike and needs context from another, the context provider must also output Quick Strike. Default assumption: speed mode is inherited. If different speed needed, both skills must explicitly sync to common speed.

Example: If Strategy Partner (QS) → Rumelt Forge (QS), SP provides crux candidates only, not full diagnosis. Rumelt selects one and builds kernel quickly.
