# Scenario Analysis for Strategy Partner

## Four-Scenario Framework for Diagnostic Confidence

This framework describes how diagnosis confidence and recommendations adjust across probability-weighted scenarios.

### UPSIDE (20% probability)
Market opportunity larger than diagnosed, customer acquisition faster, competitive threats weaker.

**Conditions:**
- TAM estimate conservative (actual +50% larger)
- Customer acquisition velocity exceeds baseline
- Competitive threats less formidable than modeled
- Macro environment supportive

**Assumptions:**
- Market willing to pay premium for differentiation
- No disruptive entrants emerging
- Macro tailwinds (economy, tech adoption)

**Output adjustments:**
- Diagnosis confidence upgrades to HIGH
- Execution timeline compresses 3-6 months
- Investment priority escalates
- Recommend exploiting market opportunity faster

---

### BASE CASE (50% probability)
Market opportunity as diagnosed, customer acquisition at expected velocity, competitive intensity normal.

**Conditions:**
- Market sizing accurate ±10%
- GTM motion works as designed
- Competitive response matches historical patterns
- Macro environment stable

**Assumptions:**
- Diagnosis assumptions validated
- Customer acquisition costs within forecast
- Competitive intensity as modeled

**Output adjustments:**
- Standard diagnosis, decision frame, execution plan as recommended
- Confidence reflects evidence quality and analytical depth

---

### DOWNSIDE (20% probability)
Market opportunity smaller than diagnosed, customer churn higher, competitive intensity increases.

**Conditions:**
- TAM estimate optimistic (actual -30% smaller)
- Customer acquisition slower or more expensive than forecast
- Competitive intensity higher than modeled
- Macro headwinds (economy, technology shift)

**Assumptions:**
- Market adoption slower than expected
- Competitor response more aggressive
- Macro headwinds persist

**Output adjustments:**
- Diagnosis confidence capped at MEDIUM
- Execution timeline extends by 2-3 quarters
- Cash runway reassessed; unit economics critical
- Recommend smaller initial investment and faster learning cycles

---

### DISRUPTIVE (10% probability)
Market fundamentally changes, disruptive entrant appears, technology or regulatory shift occurs.

**Conditions:**
- Disruptive entrant with new model emerges
- Technology shift makes assumptions obsolete
- Regulatory change invalidates positioning
- Macro recession or demand collapse

**Assumptions:**
- [Specific disruption type] becomes real faster than expected
- Market dynamics fundamentally different from diagnosis

**Output adjustments:**
- Strategy must pivot; previously planned recommendations invalid
- Crisis response required
- Return to rapid re-diagnosis
- Consider market timing and entry strategies

---

## Scenario Transition Triggers

Monitor these signals to detect scenario movement:

**Upside signals:**
- Market penetration rate exceeding forecast
- Customer churn rates lower than modeled
- Competitive win rates higher than historical
- Early adoption signals strong

**Downside signals:**
- Market penetration slower than forecast
- Customer churn rates rising
- Competitive win rates declining
- CAC/LTV ratio deteriorating

**Disruptive signals:**
- New competitor category appearing
- Technology shift invalidating assumptions
- Regulatory change materially affecting market
- Macro recession or demand shock emerging
