## Weekly Pulse Review (30 min)

- Review dashboard: Green / Amber / Red on all KPIs
- Escalate any new RED: what happened, root cause, course correction?
- Surface blockers: what's preventing progress?
- Adjustment: any tactical changes needed?

## Monthly Deep Review (2 hours)

- vs. Monthly targets: Revenue, acquisition, spend, key milestone progress
- Assumption register: Any evidence shift? Triggers approaching?
- Execution risks: Probability/impact changes? Early warnings surfacing?
- Functional reviews: Product, GTM, Ops, Finance deep-dives on critical metrics
- Course correction assessment: Stay course? Adjust? Need pivot discussion?

## Quarterly Business Review (4 hours)

- vs. Quarterly gates: Did we hit strategic milestones?
- Year-to-date performance: Revenue, growth rate, customer acquisition, retention
- Assumption validation: Which assumptions held? Which broke?
- Strategy health: Is strategy still valid or is pivot needed?
- Year-end forecast: Trajectory to annual targets, probability of success
- Resource reallocation: Any shifts in horizon 1/2/3 allocation?

## Annual Post-Mortem (if strategy fails or major assumption breaks)

- What assumption broke, and why didn't we see it coming?
- What signals did we miss or misinterpret?
- When should we have course-corrected?
- What do we do differently next cycle?
