# Execution Risk Taxonomy & Tracking

## 8-10 Risk Categories with Early Warning Indicators

| Risk Category | Definition | Early Warning Indicators | Monitoring Frequency |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Capability Gap** | Team lacks skills to execute | Hiring delays, training incomplete, key person dependencies | Weekly hiring dashboard |
| **Leadership Change** | Key exec leaves mid-execution | Resignation, signal of discontent, org instability | Monthly 1:1s with leaders |
| **Market Shift** | TAM smaller, demand weaker, shift in buyer behavior | Slower-than-forecast pipeline, customer churn acceleration, deal cycles lengthening | Weekly pipeline reviews |
| **Competitive Response** | Competitor moves faster, enters market, undercuts pricing | Competitive win-loss analysis, pricing changes, feature announcements | Bi-weekly market intelligence |
| **Tech Risk** | System implementation delays, integration failures, scalability issues | Pilot delays, bug rates, infrastructure load tests | Weekly product review |
| **Integration Risk** | M&A, partnership, system migration doesn't integrate smoothly | Deadline slips, data discrepancies, manual workarounds | Daily integration tracking |
| **Dependency Risk** | Required upstream skill output missing or delayed | Rumelt Forge late, financial modeling incomplete, org design not done | Weekly dependency tracking |
| **Capital Risk** | Funding dries up, capital call from board, burn rate accelerates | Cash runway <3 months, burn acceleration, capital raise stuck | Monthly cash dashboard |
| **Org Friction** | Organizational resistance, turf wars block execution | Low engagement scores, champion dropout, skip-level complaints | Bi-weekly pulse surveys |
| **Assumption Break** | Core strategy assumption proves false before pivot can happen | Metric crosses red flag threshold, customer feedback contradicts assumption | Weekly dashboard review |

## Risk Tracking Template

For each risk, maintain:

```
EXECUTION RISK TRACKER
═════════════════════════════════════════
Risk: [Category] — Owner: [Executive]
  Current probability: [H / M / L]
  Current impact: [H / M / L]

  Early warning indicators (monitoring):
    - Indicator 1: [Current status] vs. normal range
    - Indicator 2: [Current status] vs. normal range

  Probability trending: [↗️ increasing / → stable / ↘️ decreasing]

  Mitigation strategy: [What we're doing to prevent this]

  Course correction if triggered: [What we do if this risk materializes]

Example:
Risk: Capability Gap in key function
  Current probability: M (hiring lagging behind plan)
  Current impact: H (GTM execution dependent on this hire)

  Indicators:
    - GTM head offer acceptance: Still pending after 6 weeks (NORMAL: 1-2 weeks)
    - Pipeline velocity: Down 15% vs. forecast (NORMAL: flat or +10%)

  Probability trending: ↗️ Increasing (4 candidates have declined; market tightening)

  Mitigation: Expanded search; recruiter commission increased; interim consultant engaged

  Course correction if triggered: Delay market entry 4 weeks OR hire interim GTM lead
═════════════════════════════════════════
```

## Risk Assessment Framework

1. **Probability vs. Impact Matrix** — Plot all risks on 3x3 matrix (H/M/L both axes)
2. **Monitor continuously** — Don't wait for quarterly reviews; weekly pulse on all risks
3. **Escalate early warnings** — Don't wait for risk to materialize; act on early indicators
4. **Mitigation investment** — Allocate resources to mitigation; prevent problems before they happen
5. **Decision triggers** — When risk fires, what's the decision? Have the logic pre-defined
