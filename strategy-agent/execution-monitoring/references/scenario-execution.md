# Scenario Analysis for Execution Monitoring

## Four-Scenario Framework for Execution Health

This framework describes how execution trajectories and course correction decisions adjust across probability-weighted scenarios.

### UPSIDE (20% probability)
Execution outpaces plan, assumptions hold, higher confidence in strategy.

**Conditions:**
- All KPIs GREEN for 2+ consecutive months
- 3+ material assumptions confirmed early with supporting evidence
- No execution blockers or friction surfaces
- Team delivering ahead of schedule

**Assumptions:**
- Annual targets achieved 3+ months early
- Forecast confidence rises to 90%+
- Execution velocity sustainable

**Output adjustments:**
- Increase resource allocation to H2/H3 initiatives
- Accelerate timeline for dependent workstreams
- Capture upside optionality (expand TAM, accelerate growth targets)

**KPI adjustment:**
- Raise annual targets 15-20% based on performance evidence
- Reset quarterly gates upward
- Increase leading indicator targets

**Course correction:**
- Minimal tactical adjustments needed
- Focus on maintaining momentum and reinvesting gains
- Escalate to "accelerate" mode rather than "adjust"

**Quality level:** Strategy execution validated; proceed with confidence

---

### BASE CASE (50% probability)
Execution on plan, assumptions hold as expected, forecast confidence stable.

**Conditions:**
- KPIs tracked to plan within normal variance (<10%)
- Assumptions validated incrementally through expected validation cycles
- Standard execution risks managed per mitigation plans
- Team pace matches original forecast

**Assumptions:**
- Annual targets hit within 10% variance
- Forecast confidence 75-80% stable
- No material surprises

**Output adjustments:**
- Execute monitoring plan as designed
- Standard monthly course correction reviews
- Maintain baseline dashboard and reporting cadence

**KPI adjustment:**
- No material changes to targets or thresholds
- Standard quarterly reviews and reforecasting
- Confidence updates reflect incremental validation

**Course correction:**
- Standard tactical adjustments only (optimize CAC, refine messaging, etc.)
- Strategy remains stable; execution execution rhythm continues
- No major pivots required

**Quality level:** Strategy execution tracking as expected; maintain discipline

---

### DOWNSIDE (20% probability)
Execution lags, material assumption breaks, confidence in strategy declines.

**Conditions:**
- KPI misses for 2 consecutive quarters (delta >10%)
- MATERIAL assumption drops from HIGH to MEDIUM/LOW confidence
- Execution risks materializing (team friction, integration delays, etc.)
- Course correction already initiated but impact uncertain

**Assumptions:**
- Annual targets miss by >15%
- Forecast confidence drops to 60-70%
- Pivot likely needed within 1-2 quarters

**Output adjustments:**
- Escalate to leadership; run course correction decision tree
- Prepare pivot options and alternative strategies
- Increase monitoring frequency (weekly vs. monthly reviews)
- Flag timeline impact to downstream dependencies

**KPI adjustment:**
- Revise annual targets downward based on YTD performance
- Reassess quarterly gates with new baseline
- Tighten leading indicators (increase sensitivity)

**Course correction:**
- ADJUST or PIVOT decision needed within 30 days
- Course correction review elevated to CFO/CEO level
- Activation of contingency plans
- Potential resource reallocation

**Quality level:** Strategy at risk; course correction required

---

### DISRUPTIVE (10% probability)
Core assumption breaks, strategy invalidated, major pivot or kill needed.

**Conditions:**
- Core business model assumption proves false
- Market shift invalidates fundamental strategy premise
- Capability gap makes execution impossible
- Recovery to plan not feasible

**Assumptions:**
- Strategy will not deliver targets under any adjustment
- Fundamental change to approach required
- Market opportunity shifted materially

**Output adjustments:**
- KILL or PIVOT decision triggered
- Escalate to board/exec for strategic reset
- Restart strategy cycle from Rumelt Forge
- Potential resource redirection or shutdown

**KPI adjustment:**
- Targets meaningless under new strategy
- Restart measurement system for new direction
- Decommission existing KPI dashboard

**Course correction:**
- PIVOT or KILL path activated
- Post-mortem required to extract lessons
- Strategic reset engagement needed
- Major stakeholder communication required

**Quality level:** Strategy invalidated; requires fundamental reset

---

## Scenario Transition Triggers

Monitor these signals to detect scenario movement:

**Upside signals:**
- 2 consecutive months of GREEN KPIs
- 3+ assumptions confirmed with strong evidence
- Zero RED escalations in risk tracker
- Execution velocity increasing
- Team morale high; engagement scores up

**Downside signals:**
- 2 consecutive AMBER KPIs trending toward RED
- MATERIAL assumption confidence drops H→M or M→L
- 1-2 risks materializing simultaneously
- Execution velocity declining
- Team friction emerging (org, dependencies)

**Disruptive signals:**
- Core assumption validation attempt fails
- Market intelligence signals fundamental shift
- Execution cascade failure (multiple dependencies break)
- Competitive disruption invalidates positioning
- Macro change (recession, regulation) changes opportunity
