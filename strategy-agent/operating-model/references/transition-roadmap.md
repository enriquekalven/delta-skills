# 180-Day Transition Roadmap Template

Bridge from current to target model with minimal disruption across four phases.

## PHASE 1: FOUNDATION (Days 1-30)

**Objective:** Set governance, build change readiness, avoid disruption

### Initiatives
- Week 1: Announce target model + change narrative
- Week 1-2: Define decision charter (RACI for key decisions)
- Week 2-3: Create transition team (Change Lead + sponsor from each function)
- Week 3-4: Survey org on readiness + feedback; iterate messaging
- Week 4: Publish first version of target operating model (structure + governance)

### Milestones
- Decision charter published and socialized
- Leadership team aligned on transition narrative
- Change team established and resourced
- Org surveyed; feedback captured

### Success Metrics
- >80% of leadership team agree on decision charter
- >70% of org understands why change is happening
- Zero escalated concerns about change direction

---

## PHASE 2: DESIGN & PREPARATION (Days 31-90)

**Objective:** Design full target model; prepare org for execution

### Initiatives
- Weeks 5-7: Design target structure in detail; identify role changes
- Weeks 5-8: Design governance processes (escalation, approvals, meetings)
- Weeks 6-9: Design core operational processes (hiring, budget, planning, ops review)
- Week 8: Announce org changes (structure, reporting, new roles)
- Week 9: Capability assessment + gap planning (training, hiring, tools)
- Week 10-12: Transition planning for affected teams
- Week 12: Announce implementation timeline; address concerns

### Milestones
- Target operating model fully designed (all 6 dimensions)
- New reporting structure defined; org changes announced
- Process documentation completed and reviewed
- Capability gaps identified; hiring/training plans created
- High-risk transitions have mitigation plans

### Success Metrics
- 85%+ of affected employees understand their new role/team
- >75% confidence in new structure (survey)
- All critical process documentation complete
- Hiring + training budgets approved

---

## PHASE 3: PILOT & ITERATION (Days 91-150)

**Objective:** Test new model at scale; iterate before full roll-out

### Initiatives
- Week 13-14: Implement new governance in one business unit (pilot)
- Week 13-15: Launch new processes (strategic planning, ops review) with pilot teams
- Week 15: Gather feedback on new processes; identify friction points
- Week 16-17: Iterate governance + processes based on pilot feedback
- Week 17: Full org transition to new structure (reporting, teams, approvals)
- Week 18-19: All-hands training on new processes
- Week 19-20: First full cycle of new operational processes (planning, review, hiring)

### Milestones
- Pilot team running under new governance + processes
- Feedback gathered; iterations made
- Full org transition completed
- All teams trained on new processes
- First full ops cycle completed

### Success Metrics
- Pilot teams report improved decision speed (measure: decision cycle time)
- >75% of org report clarity on their role + decision rights
- New processes executed on cadence
- Zero critical issues from new structure

---

## PHASE 4: STABILIZATION & CONTINUOUS IMPROVEMENT (Days 151-180)

**Objective:** Stabilize new model; fix remaining friction; set up continuous improvement

### Initiatives
- Week 21-22: Address lessons learned from Phase 3
- Week 21-26: Execute capability development plan (training, hiring, tools)
- Week 23-26: Run 1-2 quarterly planning cycles under new process
- Week 25: Measure org health (culture, clarity, collaboration)
- Week 26: Set up continuous improvement process (monthly model review)

### Milestones
- All capability gaps have improvement plans in place
- New structure + processes are the "normal" way of working
- Org health scores show improvement vs. baseline
- Continuous improvement cadence established

### Success Metrics
- >80% org report clarity on role + decision rights (vs. 65% pre-transition)
- Decision cycle time reduced 30% (measure in ops reviews)
- Process adherence >85% (processes followed as designed)
- Employee engagement stable or improved
- No critical capability gaps remain unaddressed

---

## Risk Mitigation During Transition

### Key Risks to Monitor

**Risk: Key talent leaves due to role uncertainty**
- Mitigation: Weekly 1:1s during transition; clear role descriptions Week 2; early conversations with high-risk talent
- Owner: CHRO

**Risk: New structure creates handoff delays / silos**
- Mitigation: Design cross-functional processes upfront; establish escalation rules; weekly ops reviews
- Owner: COO

**Risk: Process change reduces speed / increases friction**
- Mitigation: Pilot processes before full rollout; measure cycle times weekly; iterate rapidly
- Owner: CEO/COO
