# Core Processes Design

Seven critical processes that enable strategy execution. Design these with clarity on cadence, participants, outputs, and governance.

## 1. Strategic Planning Process

The cadence and mechanism for setting direction and resource allocation.

- **Cadence:** Quarterly OKR setting? Annual strategic planning?
- **Participants:** Who sets strategy? Who aligns?
- **Output:** OKRs? Strategic initiatives? Resource plans?
- **Governance:** Who approves? How do we stay coordinated?

## 2. Operations Review Process

The regular rhythm for assessing execution and making operational decisions.

- **Cadence:** Weekly ops review? Business review?
- **Metrics reviewed:** Revenue, cost, quality, customer, execution?
- **Decision forum:** What decisions happen in this meeting?
- **Escalation trigger:** What issues escalate to CEO?

## 3. Hiring & Onboarding Process

The system for bringing talent in and ramping them to productivity.

- **Stages:** Req approval → Sourcing → Interview → Offer → Onboarding
- **Timeline:** How long should each take?
- **Approval gates:** Who approves? By role level?
- **Onboarding:** 30-60-90 day plan? Who owns?

## 4. Budget & Forecast Process

The cycle for financial planning and resource allocation.

- **Cycle:** Annual budget + rolling forecast? Bottom-up or top-down?
- **Timing:** When do we plan? When do we review?
- **Owners:** Functional owners or CEO-driven?
- **Reforecasting:** How often? When do we replan?

## 5. Customer Escalation Process

The system for resolving customer issues and managing relationships at risk.

- **Trigger:** What escalates a customer issue?
- **Path:** When does Sales escalate to CRO? When does CRO escalate to CEO?
- **Resolution:** Who can commit resources? Customer promises?
- **Follow-up:** How do we prevent recurring escalations?

## 6. Capability Development Process

How you identify, plan, and execute capability gaps.

- **Gap identification:** How do you surface capability gaps?
- **Build vs. buy vs. hire decision:** Who decides?
- **Timeline:** How long to close gaps?
- **Measurement:** How do you track capability maturity?

## 7. Decision-Making Process (for high-stakes decisions)

The framework for how important decisions get made.

- **Decision frame:** How do you structure the decision?
- **Who decides:** Single leader or consensus?
- **When do we escalate:** Defined criteria?
- **Documentation:** How do you capture decision + rationale?

## Process Design Template

For each critical process:

```
[Process Name]
  Cadence: [Frequency]
  Participants: [Roles]
  Input: [What triggers/informs this process?]
  Output: [What decision/artifact comes out?]
  Approval: [Who approves?]
  Timeline: [When does this happen?]
  Success metric: [How do we know it's working?]
```

## Integration Notes

Processes must work together. Example:
- Strategic Planning sets OKRs and priorities
- Operations Review tracks progress toward OKRs
- Hiring Process resources the teams to execute
- Budget Process allocates money to strategic initiatives
- Capability Development closes gaps needed to execute
- Customer Escalation protects strategic customer relationships
- Decision-Making Process handles exceptions and ambiguities

If processes don't align, execution breaks down and people waste energy working across process friction.
