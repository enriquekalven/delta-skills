# Succession Planning & Leadership Development Strategy

Leadership pipeline is the most critical talent capability. A company can survive bad individual contributors. It cannot survive bad leaders. Succession planning is how you build a bench of leaders ready to step up. Development is how you build it.

---

## Part 1: Leadership Inventory & 9-Box Assessment

The 9-box is the fundamental tool for leadership assessment. It measures two dimensions: performance (what they've done) and potential (what they could do).

### 9-Box Methodology

```

Y-AXIS: POTENTIAL TO LEAD (Can they succeed at the next level?)
X-AXIS: CURRENT PERFORMANCE (Are they excelling in current role?)

                    HIGH PERFORMER        SOLID PERFORMER       UNDERPERFORMER
DEVELOP IN ROLE    ┌──────────────────┬──────────────────┬──────────────────┐
                   │ High Performers  │ Growth Ready     │ Turnaround Cases │
                   │ (Stay for now)   │ (Stretch assign) │ (Coach/exit)      │
                   │ Not ready for 1+ │ May be ready in  │ Low probability  │
                   │ level jump       │ 12-18 months     │ of success        │
                   ├──────────────────┼──────────────────┼──────────────────┤
```

### Assessing Performance (X-Axis)

```
PERFORMANCE ASSESSMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DIMENSIONS (Score each 1-5):

1. DELIVERY & EXECUTION
   5 = Consistently delivers on commitments, raises bar, pushes team to excellence
   4 = Reliable, delivers on commitments, executes well
   3 = Delivers most commitments, average execution
   2 = Misses commitments frequently, execution issues
   1 = Unreliable, poor execution

2. IMPACT & INFLUENCE
   5 = Shapes strategy, influences decisions across org, high-leverage work
   4 = Influences within domain, considered for strategic decisions
   3 = Executes impact, limited influence
   2 = Limited impact, minimal influence
   1 = Low impact, isolated work

3. COLLABORATION & TEAMWORK
   5 = Builds teams, develops people, gets buy-in across functions
   4 = Works well with others, collaborative
   3 = Transactional collaboration, no major friction
   2 = Occasional conflicts, collaboration challenges
   1 = Poor collaboration, creates friction

4. CUSTOMER UNDERSTANDING (for customer-facing roles)
   5 = Deep customer insight, drives decisions based on customer needs
   4 = Strong customer understanding, advocates for customers
   3 = Adequate customer understanding
   2 = Limited customer focus
   1 = No customer orientation

OVERALL PERFORMANCE SCORE = Average of dimensions
  4.5-5.0 = High Performer
  3.5-4.4 = Solid Performer
  2.5-3.4 = Meets Expectations
  1.5-2.4 = Below Expectations
  <1.5 = Underperformer
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

### Assessing Potential (Y-Axis)

```
POTENTIAL ASSESSMENT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

DIMENSIONS (Score each 1-5):

1. LEADERSHIP CAPABILITY
   5 = Demonstrates leadership behaviors now: coach, delegate, take ownership
   4 = Shows some leadership behaviors, ready to develop more
   3 = Can step into leadership with support
   2 = Early in leadership journey, needs significant development
   1 = Not ready for leadership role

2. STRATEGIC THINKING
   5 = Thinks systemically, sees across functions, thinks 2-3 years out
   4 = Strategic thinker, but often focused on domain
   3 = Tactical thinker who can learn strategy
   2 = Primarily execution-focused, growth opportunity for strategy
   1 = No demonstrated strategic thinking

3. LEARNING AGILITY
   5 = Adapts quickly, learns from mistakes, operates in ambiguity with confidence
   4 = Learns well, adapts, handles ambiguity
   3 = Learns new things, may need structure
   2 = Learning takes time, prefers structure
   1 = Struggles to learn new things

4. EMOTIONAL INTELLIGENCE
   5 = Self-aware, manages emotions well, reads others effectively
   4 = Good emotional awareness and interpersonal skills
   3 = Adequate EI, some awareness
   2 = Limited emotional awareness, occasional interpersonal friction
   1 = Low EI, significant interpersonal challenges

5. AMBITION & DRIVE
   5 = Wants to move up, actively developing, clear growth trajectory
   4 = Interested in growth, developing
   3 = Open to growth, may not actively pursue
   2 = Comfortable in current role, not seeking growth
   1 = No ambition for growth

OVERALL POTENTIAL SCORE = Average of dimensions
  4.5-5.0 = Ready for 2+ levels above
  3.5-4.4 = Ready for next level (1-2 years)
  2.5-3.4 = Develop in current role for potential
  1.5-2.4 = Limited upward mobility
  <1.5 = No clear leadership path
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

### 9-Box Calibration & Action Planning

```
ACTIONS BY 9-BOX QUADRANT
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

HIGH-POT (High Performance + High Potential)
  Quadrant: Top-right, ready for next level
  Action: Promote within 6-12 months or risk losing them
  Development: Stretch assignment. Mentor from senior leader.
              Formal leadership training. Clear path to director/VP.
  Retention: Pay at top of range. Equity refresh. Autonomy & growth.
  Risk: Most likely to be recruited. Must develop actively.

  Example: Senior engineer, strong performance, shows leadership capability,
           interested in growing to engineering manager.
  Action: Assign as tech lead on critical project. Pair with mentor.
          Provide manager training. Promote to manager within 12 months.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

GROWTH READY (High Performance + Mid Potential)
  Quadrant: Mid-right, developing for next level
  Action: Develop in current role 12-18 months, then assess for promotion
  Development: Stretch assignment. Specific feedback on next-level requirements.
              Mentor from senior leader. Leadership training.
  Retention: Clear path to promotion, even if 18 months away. Opportunities to grow.
  Risk: May plateau or leave if no growth opportunity.

  Example: Mid-level product manager, strong execution, limited strategic thinking.
  Action: Lead strategic initiative. Get regular feedback on strategic thinking.
          Provide strategy training/mentoring.
          Re-assess in 18 months for promotion to senior PM.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

DEVELOP IN ROLE (High Performance + Low Potential)
  Quadrant: Top-left, strong but not ready for next level
  Action: Develop as senior IC. Create career path that doesn't require promotion.
  Development: Deepen expertise. Take on complex technical/functional projects.
              Mentor junior people. Lead working groups. Be the expert.
  Retention: Compensation at top of IC range. Autonomy. Impact.
  Risk: May feel stuck or leave if only path is "manage people."

  Example: Strong engineer, excellent execution, no interest in management.
  Action: Create "staff engineer" track. Lead architecture initiative.
          Mentor others. Be the technical authority. Compensate well.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

SOLID PERFORMER (Solid Performance + Mid Potential)
  Quadrant: Center, stable performer
  Action: Monitor. Provide feedback. Develop where ready.
  Development: Regular 1:1s. Feedback on performance + potential. Growth opportunities.
  Retention: Fair compensation. Growth opportunity.
  Risk: May be overlooked. Ensure they don't feel stalled.

  Example: Customer success manager, solid performance, potential to lead.
  Action: Quarterly feedback on what's needed for promotion. Assign to lead project.
          Re-assess quarterly for readiness.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

TURNAROUND CASES (High Performance + Low Potential)
  Quadrant: Top-left but with gap in leadership/strategic thinking
  Action: Specific feedback. Coaching. 90-day development plan.
  Development: Targeted coaching (executive coach, mentor). Specific behaviors.
  Retention: Show path to growth. Invest in development.
  Risk: May leave if they see no path. May not improve despite coaching.

  Example: Strong individual engineer but poor collaboration skills.
  Action: Executive coach for collaboration. Specific feedback.
          Re-assess in 90 days. If no improvement, may not be promotion path.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

CLEAR PROBLEMS (Below Performance + Low Potential)
  Quadrant: Bottom-right
  Action: Performance management. Coaching. Exit plan if no improvement.
  Development: Performance improvement plan (PIP). Clear expectations.
              90-day check-in. Clear consequences.
  Retention: Not a focus. Prepare for exit.
  Risk: Dragging down team. Acting early is humane.

  Example: Manager not hitting targets, not developing people.
  Action: 90-day PIP with specific metrics. Coach on gaps. Clear exit path if fails.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

## Part 3: Leadership Development Programs

Succession planning is passive (waiting for people to be ready). Development is active (building leaders).

### Leadership Development Framework

```
LEADERSHIP DEVELOPMENT ROADMAP
━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━

LEVEL 1: HIGH PERFORMER IC → READY FOR MANAGEMENT
  Program: "Manager Ready" (3-6 months)
  Components:
    • Management fundamentals course (hiring, feedback, 1:1s)
    • Mentor from experienced manager (monthly, 1 hour)
    • Lead small project with oversight
    • 360 feedback assessment
    • Read: "The Effective Manager" by Mark Horstman

  Success metric: Passes 360 feedback, ready for manager role

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LEVEL 2: NEW MANAGER (0-18 months as manager)
  Program: "New Manager Acceleration" (6 months, ongoing)
  Components:
    • Manager coaching: Monthly 1:1 with CHRO/mentor (topics: team dynamics,
      feedback, difficult conversations, delegation)
    • Manager training program: Weekly 90-min sessions on:
      ✓ Hiring & interviewing
      ✓ Feedback & performance management
      ✓ Delegation & delegation failures
      ✓ Decision-making & prioritization
      ✓ Team meetings & culture
      ✓ Handling underperformers
    • Peer group: Monthly lunch with other new managers (sharing challenges,
      solutions)
    • 360 feedback at 6 months & 12 months

  Success metric: Team engagement scores improve. No major turnover.
                 360 shows management competency growth.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LEVEL 3: MANAGER → DIRECTOR/SENIOR MANAGER
  Program: "Leadership Impact" (12 months)
  Components:
    • Executive coaching (bi-weekly, 6 months focus on strategic thinking,
      influence, decision-making)
    • Strategy deep-dive: Quarterly meetings with CEO on business strategy.
      Manager leads one strategic initiative.
    • Cross-functional leadership: Co-lead initiative with peer from another function
    • Board exposure: CEO introduces to board members. Manager presents to board.
    • Leadership assessment: Custom assessment of strengths/gaps
    • Reading group: Quarterly discussion of leadership book
    • External program: Leadership program (e.g., SVP, YPO, GLG roundtable)

  Success metric: 360 shows director-level competency. Ready for promotion.
                 Demonstrates strategic thinking & influence.

────────────────────────────────────────────────────────────

LEVEL 4: DIRECTOR → VP/C-LEVEL
  Program: "Executive Development" (12-24 months)
  Components:
    • Executive coach (monthly, 2-year engagement)
    • CEO 1:1s: Weekly/bi-weekly with CEO (strategic, decision-making,
      leadership challenges)
    • Board exposure: Attend board meetings. Present business unit to board.
    • Industry peer group: Join CEO peer group (Junto, YPO, etc.)
    • Reading & learning: Executive education program (Harvard, Stanford, etc.)
    • Succession planning: CEO prepares for larger role or exit.
    • 360 & psychometric assessment: Understand leadership presence, blind spots

  Success metric: 360 shows VP-level competency. Ready for C-level role.
                 Demonstrates strategic ownership & executive presence.

━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━━
```

## Quality Gates for Succession & Leadership Strategy

**Gate 1: Real Assessment** — Is the 9-box based on data (performance reviews, 360 feedback, manager input) or gut feel? Gut assessments are often wrong. Use data.

**Gate 2: Specificity** — Are succession plans specific (names, timelines, development actions) or vague? "We'll develop someone" means nothing. "We'll develop [Name] as engineering manager through [specific steps] by [date]" means action.

**Gate 3: Development Rigor** — Are development programs in place or aspirational? "We should train managers" doesn't develop managers. Structured programs with accountability do.

**Gate 4: Retention Link** — Are high-pots actively retained (equity, autonomy, growth path) or just identified? Identifying without retaining doesn't build bench. Retention must be intentional.

**Gate 5: Board Visibility** — Does the board see succession plans quarterly? If succession isn't on the board agenda, it's not a priority. Make it visible.
