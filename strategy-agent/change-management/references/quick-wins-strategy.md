# Quick Wins Strategy

Identify and sequence early wins (60-120 days) that prove change is real and build momentum.

## Quick Wins Criteria

Select wins that meet ALL of these:

| Criterion | Why It Matters | Example |
|-----------|---|---|
| **Achievable in 60 days** | Proves change is real, not theoretical | One process redesigned (not entire transformation) |
| **Visible to whole org** | Everyone sees it, builds credibility | Affects > 20% of org |
| **Proves new way works** | Demonstrates value proposition | Shows efficiency gain or capability unlock |
| **Builds credibility** | Leader-backed, celebrated org-wide | CEO personally celebrates win; story shared widely |

## Quick Wins Identification Process

**For each proposed quick win, ask:**

1. Can we deliver in 60 days with dedicated resources?
   - If NO → Break into smaller win
   - If YES → Proceed

2. Will everyone see it? Or just one team?
   - If just one team → Not visible enough; find bigger win
   - If org-wide visibility → Proceed

3. Does it prove new way works?
   - If YES → Customers see efficiency, or team sees capability gain
   - If NO → It's nice-to-have, not quick win; move to later phase

4. Can we celebrate it org-wide?
   - If YES → Plan celebration; get CEO commitment
   - If NO → Skip it; find win we can celebrate

## Quick Wins by Change Type

**PROCESS CHANGE:**
- One process redesigned, cycle time -50%, all customers see it
- Timeline: 6-8 weeks
- Cost: $20-40K
- Example: "Hiring cycle reduced from 60 days to 30 days"

**SYSTEM IMPLEMENTATION:**
- One department live, productivity +20%, case study published
- Timeline: 8-10 weeks
- Cost: $50-100K
- Example: "New CRM live in Sales; deal velocity increased 30%"

**RESTRUCTURE:**
- New org chart live, retained key talent, filled key roles
- Timeline: 4-6 weeks
- Cost: $10-20K (announcements, 1:1s)
- Example: "New customer-centric structure live; no key departures"

**CULTURE SHIFT:**
- Peer recognition program launched, 20+ peer shout-outs in first month
- Timeline: 2-4 weeks
- Cost: $5-10K (platform + launch)
- Example: "Peer recognition program active; 50+ recognitions in month 1"

**CAPABILITY BUILD:**
- New skill training launched, 100% completion in cohort
- Timeline: 4-6 weeks
- Cost: $30-50K (training + facilitation)
- Example: "Leadership training complete; 95% of managers through new program"

## Quick Wins Sequencing

**Weeks 1-4:** Foundation wins (build credibility of change)
- Goal: Prove leadership is serious and can deliver
- Example: Decision charter published; first RACI decision made
- Impact: "Leadership isn't just talking; they're actually changing how they decide"

**Weeks 5-8:** Visible wins (prove new way works)
- Goal: Show that change delivers promised benefit
- Example: Process redesigned; 50% cycle time reduction
- Impact: "This new way actually works! I can see it in [visible metric]"

**Weeks 9-12:** Expansion wins (build momentum)
- Goal: Show that change applies to whole org, not just pilot
- Example: Second process redesigned; new process now used in 3 teams
- Impact: "Change is spreading; it's becoming how we work"

## Celebration & Communication Plan

**For each quick win:**

1. **Announce the win** (All-hands email, town hall)
   - What was the win?
   - How does it prove new way works?
   - Who contributed?

2. **Celebrate the people** (Recognition event)
   - CEO gives shout-out
   - Feature in company newsletter
   - Peer recognition

3. **Share the story** (Narrative + data)
   - Before/after metrics
   - Customer testimonial
   - Team quote about how new way works

4. **Reinforce the lesson** (Message for next phase)
   - "This is the power of [change principle]"
   - "Imagine when this applies across whole org"
   - "This is what we're building toward"

## Common Pitfalls to Avoid

**Pitfall 1: Picking wins that are too small**
- Problem: "We updated the org chart" as a quick win doesn't prove new way works
- Fix: Pick wins that show tangible value (faster, cheaper, better quality)

**Pitfall 2: Picking wins that only one group sees**
- Problem: New process works great in Sales, but Engineering doesn't know about it
- Fix: Pick wins with org-wide visibility

**Pitfall 3: Forgetting to celebrate**
- Problem: You deliver quick wins but don't make a big deal out of them
- Fix: Plan celebration before you start. Get CEO commitment to celebrate.

**Pitfall 4: Picking too many wins**
- Problem: You try 10 quick wins and none get proper attention
- Fix: Pick 5-7 wins max. Celebrate each one heavily.

**Pitfall 5: Declaring victory too early**
- Problem: You celebrate wins and people assume change is done
- Fix: Use wins to build momentum to next phase. "This is just the beginning."
