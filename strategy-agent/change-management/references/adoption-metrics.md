# Adoption Metrics & Monitoring Framework

Track adoption with weekly and monthly metrics. Use data to detect resistance early and adjust course.

## Weekly Pulse Metrics (10-Question Survey)

Run this survey every Friday with 10-15 random employees:

```
1. Awareness: Do you understand what's changing? (1-5 scale)
2. Necessity: Is this change needed? (1-5 scale)
3. Capability confidence: Can you succeed in new way? (1-5 scale)
4. Leadership credibility: Do you trust leadership to execute? (1-5 scale)
5. Adoption intent: Will you adopt the new way? (1-5 scale)
6. Perceived barrier: What's the biggest blocker for you? (open-ended)
7. Adoption: Have you used/done the new way? (Yes/No)
8. Benefit: Has change delivered promised benefits? (1-5 scale)
9. Recommendation: Would you recommend this change to others? (1-5 scale)
10. Support: Have you received help/training? (Yes/No)
```

## Monthly Deep Metrics (functional leadership reviews)

**Every 4 weeks, leadership team reviews:**

- **Adoption rate by population segment** (%) — Are all groups moving together?
- **Training completion rate** (%) — Are people going through training?
- **Resistance intensity by segment** (high/medium/low) — Which groups are struggling?
- **Champion effectiveness** (# of peer conversions per champion) — Are champions actually influencing?
- **Capability gaps** (% saying "I can't do this"; by role) — Where do we need more support?
- **Process compliance** (% following new way in daily work) — Is new way actually being used?
- **Business metrics** (is change delivering promised outcome?) — Is this working?

## Resistance Monitoring & Mitigation

**Weekly Resistance Pulse:**

```
Which segments are resisting most? (Score 1-10 by function)
What are the top 3 stated barriers? (Categorize: capability, incentive, fear, clarity)
Who are the power influencers against change? (Name them; assess influence)
Are resisters isolated or coalescing? (Assess network effects)
```

**Course Correction Triggers:**

| Metric | Red Flag | Amber Flag | Action |
|--------|----------|-----------|--------|
| **Weekly Adoption Intent** | <30% | 30-50% | Relaunch change narrative; address top barrier |
| **Capability Confidence** | <40% | 40-60% | Deploy additional training; assign peer coaches |
| **Resistance Intensity** | High, growing | High, stable | Meet resisters 1:1; reframe change logic; offer alternative path |
| **Champion Dropout** | >20% churn | 10-20% | Address burnout; celebrate champions; rotate roles |
| **Adoption Rate Trajectory** | Flat or declining | <50% by week 12 | Kill initiative OR pivot approach (slower, more support) |

## Monthly Change Plan Review & Adjustment

**Every 4 weeks, leadership team reviews:**

1. **Adoption Dashboard:** Track vs. targets
2. **Resistance Heat Map:** Which functions/roles resisting most?
3. **Early Wins:** Delivered? Celebrated? Impact measured?
4. **Communication Effectiveness:** Is message landing? Awareness trending up? Sentiment shift?
5. **Capability Gaps:** Training working? Skill building on pace?
6. **Go/No-Go Decision:** Stay on course? Adjust? Pivot? Kill?

## Adjustment Matrix

**If adoption < 50% at Week 12:**
- **Option A:** Slow timeline; add 4 weeks for capability building
- **Option B:** Pivot approach (lighter change, broader support)
- **Option C:** Kill initiative; document learnings

**If adoption 50-70% at Week 12:**
- **Option A:** Maintain course; tighten reinforcement
- **Option B:** Increase champion investment; reduce resistance risks

**If adoption > 70% at Week 12:**
- **Proceed:** Move to full-scale rollout; lock in new behaviors via systems/incentives
