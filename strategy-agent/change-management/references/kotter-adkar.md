# Kotter's 8-Step Model & ADKAR Framework

Two complementary frameworks for designing and executing organizational change.

## Kotter's 8-Step Model (Operationalized)

| Step | Definition | Specific Tactics | Timeline | Owner |
|------|-----------|-----------------|----------|-------|
| **1. Urgency** | Create compelling case for change—why now? | Quantified problem, market threat, competitive comparison, executive town hall | Weeks 1-2 | Exec sponsor |
| **2. Coalition** | Build exec + stakeholder guiding coalition | Identify champions by function, executive steering committee (weekly), change ambassador network | Weeks 2-4 | Change lead + PMO |
| **3. Vision & Strategy** | Crystallize what "future state" looks like | Concise 1-page vision, 3-5 year blueprint, success metrics at year 1/2/3 | Weeks 3-4 | Strategy + change leads |
| **4. Communicate Vision** | Cascade vision to org, build desire | Tiered comms, Q&A forums, peer testimonials, 7-9 touchpoints in first 90 days | Weeks 4-12 | Comms team + leaders |
| **5. Empower Action** | Remove barriers, enable experimentation | Dedicated resources, simplified approval, quick-win pilots, protected time | Weeks 5-16 | PMO + functional leads |
| **6. Quick Wins** | Sequence early, high-impact wins; celebrate | 60-day wins proving change works, visible org-wide, celebrate success weekly | Weeks 5-12 | Program team |
| **7. Build Momentum** | Keep change going; avoid backslide | Continuous communication, champion program grows, process changes lock in behaviors | Weeks 12-24 | Exec sponsor + PMO |
| **8. Anchor in Culture** | Make change permanent | New hiring criteria, onboarding teaches new behaviors, stories embedded in culture, leadership evaluations include change leadership | Month 6-24 | HR + exec team |

## ADKAR Model (Adoption Architecture)

Design specific interventions for each adoption phase:

```
AWARENESS → DESIRE → KNOWLEDGE → ABILITY → REINFORCEMENT
   (Know why)  (Want to)   (Know how)   (Can do)   (Will stay)
```

### AWARENESS
**Goal:** Do people know change is coming? Do they understand why?

**What people ask:** "What's changing?" "Why?" "Does it affect me?"

**Intervention strategy:**
- Town halls explaining change
- Email cascades from leadership
- One-on-one leader conversations
- Problem/opportunity articulation (the case for change)
- Competitive data (what happens if we don't change)
- Personal impact analysis (how does change affect "me")

**Target:** 70%+ awareness by week 4, 90%+ by week 8

### DESIRE
**Goal:** Do people want to participate? Is it compelling enough to overcome inertia?

**What people ask:** "What's in it for me?" "Will I be OK?" "Can we succeed?"

**Intervention strategy:**
- Peer testimonials (people like me say this is good)
- Small wins proof points (it's already working for this group)
- Leadership credibility (do leaders believe in this?)
- Fear-addressing Q&A (safe space to voice concerns)
- Personal benefit articulation (here's how you win)

**Target:** 40%+ desire by week 4, 60%+ by week 8, 75%+ by week 12

### KNOWLEDGE
**Goal:** Do people know HOW to operate in the new way? Do they have skills, processes, systems?

**What people ask:** "How do I do this?" "Where do I get help?" "What if I fail?"

**Intervention strategy:**
- Role-specific training (not one-size-fits-all)
- Playbooks and job aids (documentation)
- Peer coaches (experienced people helping new people)
- Just-in-time learning (help when you need it, not weeks before)
- Safe practice environments (chance to try before going live)

**Target:** 80%+ training completion by week 12, 90%+ by week 16

### ABILITY
**Goal:** Can people actually execute the new way? Do they have resources, support, space to learn?

**What people ask:** "I'm struggling." "Is this normal?" "What's success?"

**Intervention strategy:**
- Embedded coaches in teams (daily support)
- Support forums (help channels, peer learning)
- Iteration on training (based on what people are struggling with)
- Normalize experimentation (safe to try, fail, learn)
- Celebrate learning mistakes (show that trying and failing is OK)

**Target:** 60%+ adopting new way by week 12, 75%+ by week 16

### REINFORCEMENT
**Goal:** Will the new way stick? Are systems, incentives, and culture locked in?

**What people ask:** "Is this permanent?" "What happens if I slip back?" "Who are role models?"

**Intervention strategy:**
- Incentive alignment (reward new behaviors, not old)
- New process becomes "only way" (systems lock in new behavior)
- Hiring/firing signals (new people hired for new skills; old way people might be at risk)
- Measurement visibility (track metrics everyone can see)
- Celebrate consistent behavior (recognize people embodying new way)

**Target:** 80%+ sustaining new way by month 6, 90%+ by month 12

## Integration: Kotter + ADKAR

Kotter provides the macro structure (8 steps). ADKAR provides the micro psychology (what interventions move each person through adoption).

**Mapping:**
- Kotter step 1 (Urgency) → ADKAR Awareness
- Kotter step 4 (Communicate Vision) → ADKAR Desire
- Kotter step 5 (Empower Action) → ADKAR Knowledge + Ability
- Kotter step 8 (Anchor in Culture) → ADKAR Reinforcement

**Use together:** Kotter gives you the roadmap (what to do when). ADKAR gives you the interventions (how to move people psychologically).
