# Scenario Analysis for Rumelt Strategy Forge

## Four-Scenario Framework for Strategy Viability

This framework describes how strategy viability and kernel confidence adjust across probability-weighted scenarios.

### UPSIDE (20% probability)
Strategy more viable than diagnosed, market favorable, execution easier.

**Conditions:**
- Crux diagnosis correct and validated
- Guiding policy exploits real competitive asymmetry
- Market conditions favor the strategy
- Team capabilities exceed baseline assumptions
- Competitive response weaker than modeled

**Assumptions:**
- Market adoption faster than plan
- Guiding policy creates stronger moat than expected
- Execution team performs above baseline

**Output adjustments:**
- Kernel confidence upgrades to HIGH
- Scaling timeline accelerates 3-6 months
- Coherence score improves (>80/100 likely)
- Recommend accelerating resource allocation to early-winning actions

---

### BASE CASE (50% probability)
Strategy viability as diagnosed, crux is real, execution follows plan.

**Conditions:**
- Diagnosis valid and assumptions hold
- Team has required capabilities
- Competitive response matches modeled expectations
- Market timing aligns with forecast

**Assumptions:**
- Crux identification correct
- Guiding policy creates intended competitive advantage
- Execution team resourced appropriately

**Output adjustments:**
- Standard kernel with coherence score as calculated
- Recommendations proceed as designed
- Confidence reflects diagnostic base + validation tests

---

### DOWNSIDE (20% probability)
Strategy viability challenged, crux may be secondary, competitive response fierce.

**Conditions:**
- Key assumption from diagnosis proves incorrect
- Execution friction emerges (capability gap, integration complexity)
- Market adoption slower than plan
- Competitive response more aggressive than modeled
- Key business model assumption weakens

**Assumptions:**
- Crux diagnosis may be incomplete or secondary
- Execution capability gap emerges
- Market opportunity smaller or slower than modeled

**Output adjustments:**
- Kernel confidence capped at MEDIUM
- Actions may need resequencing (de-prioritize risky moves)
- Kill triggers move closer and become more sensitive
- Recommend contingency planning and scenario hedging

---

### DISRUPTIVE (10% probability)
Strategy fundamentally invalidated, crux changes, competitive landscape shifts.

**Conditions:**
- Crux diagnosis invalidated by new information
- Market dynamics shift materially
- Competitive landscape changes (new entrant, disruptive move)
- Regulatory or macro change negates strategic assumptions
- Technology shift undermines core positioning

**Assumptions:**
- New constraint is more binding than diagnosed crux
- Market opportunity fundamentally changed
- Competitive positioning assumptions invalid

**Output adjustments:**
- Strategy must be abandoned
- Return to Strategy Partner for re-diagnosis
- New crux required
- Kernel cannot be salvaged with minor adjustments

---

## Scenario Transition Triggers

Monitor these signals to detect scenario movement:

**Upside indicators:**
- Crux indicators confirming real competitive gap
- Market adoption tracking ahead of baseline forecast
- Team execution capability exceeding expectations
- Competitive response weaker than modeled

**Downside indicators:**
- Key assumption from diagnosis weakening in evidence
- Execution friction emerging (capability gap, dependencies)
- Market adoption slower than modeled
- Competitive response more aggressive than expected

**Disruptive indicators:**
- Core business model assumption invalidated
- New binding constraint identified (not the diagnosed crux)
- Market intelligence signals fundamental category shift
- Competitive disruption emerging from unexpected direction
