# Intelligence Synthesis & Pattern Recognition

## Intelligence Synthesis Template

When you have multiple signals, synthesize them:

```
INTELLIGENCE SYNTHESIS TEMPLATE
═════════════════════════════════════════
Topic: [What we're synthesizing]
Time period: [Over what timeframe are we seeing patterns?]

SIGNALS COLLECTED
  Signal 1: [Specific observation] | Source: [Tier X] | Recency: [Date] | Confidence: [H/M/L]
  Signal 2: [Specific observation] | Source: [Tier X] | Recency: [Date] | Confidence: [H/M/L]
  Signal 3: [Specific observation] | Source: [Tier X] | Recency: [Date] | Confidence: [H/M/L]

PATTERN RECOGNITION
  What story do these 3+ signals tell together? [Narrative]

  Is this:
    - Coordinated strategy move (multiple signals aligned to one strategy) OR
    - Reactive responses (responding to market pressure) OR
    - Experimental probes (testing direction, not committed) OR
    - Noise (unrelated events coinciding) OR
    - False pattern (bias-driven story we're creating)

CONFIDENCE ASSESSMENT
  Given the signals:
    - Confidence in pattern: [H / M / L]
    - Confidence in interpretation: [H / M / L]
    - Confidence in competitor's commitment: [H / M / L] (Will they stick with this?)

STRATEGIC IMPLICATIONS
  If this pattern is real, what does it mean for us?
    - Threat? [Specific threat if true]
    - Opportunity? [Specific opportunity if true]
    - Irrelevant? [Why we shouldn't care]

  What's the time horizon?
    - Immediate (1-3 months): [What we should do now]
    - Medium-term (3-12 months): [What we should prepare for]
    - Long-term (1+ years): [What we should watch]

  What would invalidate this pattern?
    - If [event] happens, pattern is false → What do we do then?

Example Synthesis:
Signals:
  1. Competitor A hired head of partnerships (LinkedIn) | Tier 3 | 2 weeks ago
  2. Competitor A announced partnership with distribution partner (press release) | Tier 2 | This week
  3. Competitor A paused self-service sales, focused on enterprise (win-loss) | Tier 1 | Last month

Pattern: Competitor A is pivoting to enterprise go-to-market via partnerships

Implication: They're going after our largest accounts; they'll move slower (6+ months) but will have distribution advantage
Our response: Solidify top 10 account relationships now; consider strategic partner discussions

═════════════════════════════════════════
```

## Intelligence Brief Format

For each key question, produce a 1-page brief:

```
EXECUTIVE INTELLIGENCE BRIEF
═════════════════════════════════════════
Classification: INTELLIGENCE BRIEF | COMPETITIVE | CUSTOMER | MARKET TREND
Date: [Prepared on]
Subject: [Specific topic]
Confidence Level: [HIGH / MEDIUM / LOW]

BOTTOM LINE
[1 sentence — what you need to know right now]

SITUATION
[2-3 sentences on the current state based on signals]

SIGNALS & EVIDENCE
  Signal 1: [What we observed + source + confidence] → Implication: [What it means]
  Signal 2: [What we observed + source + confidence] → Implication: [What it means]
  Signal 3: [What we observed + source + confidence] → Implication: [What it means]

ANALYSIS
[3-4 sentences synthesizing signals into strategic interpretation]

IMPLICATIONS FOR US
  Threat: [Specific threat if pattern is real]
  Opportunity: [Specific opportunity if pattern is real]
  Timeline: [When does this matter? 1-3mo / 3-12mo / 1+yr]

RECOMMENDED ACTIONS
  1. [Specific action + owner + timeline]
  2. [Specific action + owner + timeline]

CONFIDENCE & CAVEATS
  Confidence basis: [Why we believe this at stated confidence level]
  Key uncertainties: [What could prove us wrong]
  Next validation: [When/how we confirm or refute pattern] | [Date]

SOURCES
  Primary: [Tier 1 sources used]
  Secondary: [Tier 2 sources used]
═════════════════════════════════════════
```

## Competitor Playbook

Maintain a living playbook for each key competitor:

```
COMPETITOR PLAYBOOK
═════════════════════════════════════════
Competitor: [Name]
Last updated: [Date]
Confidence in this assessment: [H / M / L]

STRATEGIC POSITIONING
  Mission/vision: [What they claim + what signals suggest]
  Market focus: [Primary segments / geographies / use cases]
  Differentiation: [How they position vs. us / vs. category]
  Trend: [Shifting focus? Doubling down? Pivoting?]

PRODUCT STRATEGY
  Core offering: [What they sell]
  Key features (for winning): [Top 3 features in win-loss]
  Roadmap signals: [What's coming next based on hiring + partnerships]
  Weaknesses (per customers): [Top 3 complaints]

GO-TO-MARKET
  Sales motion: [How they sell — direct, self-serve, partners]
  Pricing: [Price point, packaging, discounting tendency]
  Customer acquisition: [CAC estimate, channel mix]
  Customer base: [Estimated ARR, top customers (if public), growth]

FINANCIAL HEALTH
  Revenue estimate: [Based on analyst data, public filings, industry benchmarks]
  Growth rate: [YoY growth estimate, confidence: H/M/L]
  Profitability: [Path to profitability or burn rate]
  Funding/capital: [Runway estimate, next funding likely when?]

COMPETITIVE THREAT ASSESSMENT
  Short-term (1-3mo): [What are they doing that affects us?]
  Medium-term (3-12mo): [What roadmap items threaten us?]
  Long-term (1+yr): [Strategic threat if they succeed]

OUR DEFENSIVE PLAYBOOK
  Account defense: [How we protect our customers from competitive moves]
  Feature response: [What we need to build to stay competitive]
  Pricing response: [How we adjust pricing if they undercut]
  Market response: [How we position against their claims]

NEXT INTELLIGENCE PRIORITIES
  1. [What we need to confirm about them]
  2. [What's still unknown]
  3. [How often we should update this playbook] — Every [period]
═════════════════════════════════════════
```
