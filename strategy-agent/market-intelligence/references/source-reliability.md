# Source Reliability Framework & Strategy

## Source Tier System

**Tier 1 (Verified, high reliability, but slower)**
- Company financial statements (SEC filings, earnings transcripts)
  - Reliability: 95% | Freshness: Quarterly | Bias risk: Low | Cost: Free
  - What we can learn: Revenue, profit margin, customer concentration, guidance

- Customer win-loss interviews (structured, 20+ deals)
  - Reliability: 85% | Freshness: Real-time | Bias risk: Medium (sample bias) | Cost: $10K+
  - What we can learn: Why we win/lose vs. competitor, pricing perception, feature gaps

**Tier 2 (High quality, 60-80% reliable, moderate speed)**
- Analyst reports (Gartner, Forrester, IDC)
  - Reliability: 75% | Freshness: Annual/biannual | Bias risk: Medium (vendor influence) | Cost: $5K-20K
  - What we can learn: Market sizing, trend analysis, competitive positioning, customer perception

- Press releases and company announcements
  - Reliability: 80% | Freshness: Real-time | Bias risk: High (marketing spin) | Cost: Free
  - What we can learn: Strategic moves, partnerships, product launches, hiring signals

**Tier 3 (Fast, but need cross-reference, 50-70% reliable)**
- Industry publications and newsletters
  - Reliability: 65% | Freshness: Daily/weekly | Bias risk: Medium | Cost: $500-2K
  - What we can learn: Trends, company moves, personnel changes, funding announcements

- LinkedIn signals (job postings, hiring patterns, employee moves)
  - Reliability: 60% | Freshness: Real-time | Bias risk: Medium (indicates direction, not certainty) | Cost: Free
  - What we can learn: Strategic hiring, talent drain, expansion signals, leadership changes

**Tier 4 (Real-time but low reliability, use for early signals only)**
- Social media (Twitter, Reddit, Product Hunt)
  - Reliability: 30% | Freshness: Real-time | Bias risk: High | Cost: Free
  - What we can learn: Customer sentiment, early product feedback, emerging criticism

## Key Principle: Source Reliability is Everything

Never cite Tier 4 without Tier 1 validation. Always cross-reference critical signals with at least 2 independent sources of similar tier.
