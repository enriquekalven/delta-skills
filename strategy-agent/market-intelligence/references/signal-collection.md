# Signal Collection Matrix & Customer Sentiment

## Competitive Signal Taxonomy

Map every type of competitive signal you might encounter:

### STRATEGIC SIGNALS (What's the competitor's big move?)

**Product strategy:**
- Feature launches (What? When? Target customer?)
- Product sunsetting (What? When? Migration strategy?)
- Platform pivots (Core product redesign? New direction?)

**Go-to-market:**
- Pricing changes (Price point? Packaging? Discounting?)
- Sales team expansion (Headcount? Regions? Segments?)
- Channel partnerships (New channel? Distribution strategy?)

**Investment/Resources:**
- Funding announcements (Amount? Valuation? Use of proceeds?)
- M&A moves (Acquisition? Target? Strategic rationale?)
- Hiring patterns (Roles? Headcount? Departments?)

### COMPETITIVE POSITION SIGNALS (How are they doing vs. us?)

**Customer acquisition:**
- Win-loss vs. your company (Losing deals? Winning deals? Why?)
- Customer concentration (Top customers growing? Churning?)
- Geographic expansion (New markets? Entry strategy?)

**Financial health:**
- Revenue/growth signals (Analyst estimates? Guidance? Growth rate?)
- Margin trends (Pricing power? Cost discipline?)
- Burn rate (Profitability? Runway?)

**Product health:**
- Customer satisfaction (NPS estimates from reviews? Support forums?)
- Feature adoption (What features are customers using most?)
- Churn indicators (Customer complaints? Negative reviews? Support load?)

### MARKET CONDITION SIGNALS (How is the market changing?)

**Demand signals:**
- Customer behavior shifts (Budget allocation changing? Buying criteria shifting?)
- Willingness to pay (Are prices sticking? Discounting pressure?)
- Segment emergence (New use cases? New buyer personas?)

**Competitive landscape:**
- New entrants (Startup disruption? Adjacent player moving in?)
- Consolidation (M&A increasing? Market concentration?)
- Substitute threats (Alternative solutions emerging?)

**External shocks:**
- Regulatory changes (Compliance burden? New market openings?)
- Technology shifts (AI? Consolidation to cloud? Open source?)
- Macro changes (Recession signals? Budget compression?)

## Signal Scoring

For each signal:
- **Signal strength:** HIGH (multiple sources, recent, specific) / MEDIUM / LOW
- **Credibility:** HIGH (Tier 1 source) / MEDIUM (Tier 2) / LOW (Tier 3-4, needs validation)
- **Actionability:** HIGH (should change what we do) / MEDIUM (interesting) / LOW (confirmation)

## Customer Sentiment & Signal Tracking

Track what customers are saying on multiple channels:

### SENTIMENT TRACKING (Monthly)

**Product reviews (G2, Capterra, Gartner Peer Reviews)**
- Metrics: NPS by vendor, feature sentiment, churn reasons
- Frequency: Weekly scrape and categorization
- Action if <50 NPS: Product gap analysis needed

**Support forums and communities (Stack Overflow, subreddits, user forums)**
- Metrics: Problem frequency, dissatisfaction patterns, feature requests
- Frequency: Daily monitoring, weekly synthesis
- Action if spike in churn-related complaints: Customer success investigation

**Social media listening (Twitter, LinkedIn comments)**
- Metrics: Sentiment trend, complaint categories, praise categories
- Frequency: Daily monitoring
- Action if negative sentiment spike: PR/customer response needed

### CHURN PREDICTION SIGNALS

Track these early warning behaviors:
- Support ticket volume increasing 3x (early warning)
- Power user engagement declining (30-day metric)
- Failed login attempts increasing (product stability issue)
- Contract renewal slipping (deal risk)
- Executive sponsor leaving (political risk)

**Action:** If 2+ behaviors present, trigger proactive outreach + save play

### DEMAND SIGNAL TRACKING

Track these market-level demand indicators:
- Search volume for [your solution] trending up/down (Google Trends, keyword research)
- Analyst interest in category (Gartner, Forrester report frequency)
- Customer budget allocation (Survey data + win-loss insights)
- Price elasticity (Are discounts needed to win? Market saturation signal)
