## Key Frameworks at a Glance

### The 7 Types of M&A (strategic-rationale.md)
1. **Consolidation** — Roll up fragmented market, extract cost synergies
2. **Capability Acquisition** — Acquire tech/talent to avoid long build
3. **Market Entry** — Enter new market via customer relationships and distribution
4. **Vertical Integration** — Acquire supplier/distributor to improve margins
5. **Technology/Platform** — Acquire platform or IP for competitive advantage
6. **Talent Acquisition** — Acqui-hire to get specific skills in-house
7. **Financial Engineering** — Buy undervalued asset, improve operations, resell

### The Screening Matrix (target-screening.md)
- **Strategic Fit** (0-5): Does this solve your strategic problem?
- **Financial Profile** (Pass/Fail): Revenue size, profitability, growth, working capital
- **Integration Complexity** (1-5): How hard to integrate? (1=easy, 5=very hard)
- **Cultural Fit** (1-5): Will the org mesh? (1=clash, 5=perfect alignment)

### Synergy Categories (synergy-modeling.md)
- **Revenue**: Cross-sell, platform expansion, market entry, pricing power
- **Cost**: Overhead elimination, G&A consolidation, procurement, facilities
- **Capability**: Operational improvements, best practice adoption
- **Financial**: Cost of capital, working capital optimization

### The 5 DD Workstreams (due-diligence.md)
1. **Commercial**: Market position, customers, sales, growth
2. **Financial**: Revenue quality, profitability, balance sheet
3. **Operational**: IT, processes, supply chain, facilities
4. **Legal**: Contracts, IP, litigation, compliance
5. **Cultural**: Org depth, talent, leadership, values

### Integration Models (pmi-integration.md)
- **Absorption**: Full integration (consolidation deals)
- **Preservation**: Light-touch, keep independence (capability deals)
- **Symbiosis**: Selective integration (complement deals)
- **Holding**: Standalone (financial/portfolio deals)

### The 5 Experts (mixture-of-experts.md)
1. **Investment Banker**: Valuation, market dynamics, deal execution
2. **Corp Dev VP**: Strategic fit, synergies, integration, readiness
3. **PE Operating Partner**: Operational value, management, downside protection
4. **Integration PMO Lead**: Execution risk, people, customers, synergy realization
5. **Antitrust Counsel**: Regulatory risk, remedies, timeline

### Synergy Probability Weighting
- Cost synergies: 60-80% probability of realization
- Revenue synergies: 30-50% probability of realization
- Capability synergies: 70-80% probability of realization

### Integration Timeline Targets
- Cost synergies: 50% Year 1, 100% Year 2+
- Revenue synergies: 30-50% Year 1, ramp in Year 2-3
- Overall integration: 12-24 months (depending on model)

- Budget 10-20% of deal value in one-time integration costs
- Common one-time costs: severance, systems migration, professional services

## Red Flags at a Glance

### Deal Killers (Walk Away)
- Customer concentration >50% with month-to-month contracts
- Key talent departing pre-close (3+ of top 20)
- Revenue quality is 20%+ lower than reported
- Regulatory approval deemed unlikely with value-destructive remedies
- Price exceeds walk-away threshold and won't negotiate

### Yellow Flags (Mitigate Before Closing)
- Customer concentration 40-50%
- High leadership turnover (>15% YoY)
- Synergies dependent on revenue >60% of total
- No retention agreements in place
- Integration complexity >3.5 AND strategic fit <4
